2. Information We Handle

Information processed depends on whether you play as a guest, sign in, use rankings or watch ads. We or our service providers may process:

  • Google account details: your Firebase user identifier and, when provided by Google Sign-In, your name, email address and profile photo URL.
  • Game and account records: unlocked levels, completed puzzles, daily-challenge activity, scores or completion records, coin and life balances, reward activity, and settings, to the extent those features save these values.
  • Guest and device-stored data: progress and preferences stored on your device, including through localStorage.
  • Profile and ranking details: display name, profile image or selected decorative photo frame, and ranking or score information used in the profile or leaderboard interface. Public visibility is explained in Section 5.
  • Advertising and device data: IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, advertising and device identifiers, ad views and other interactions, and diagnostic information, as processed by Google AdMob.
  • Requests and correspondence: information you provide in support, privacy-rights and account-deletion requests.

We do not ask players to submit payment-card details through the Game. This statement does not cover information you independently provide to third-party websites.

3. Google Sign-In and Firebase

When you choose Google Sign-In, Google authentication services and Firebase Authentication help authenticate your account. Firebase Cloud Firestore can store account-linked progress and other game records so they can be retrieved across sessions or devices.

Google Sign-In may provide an account identifier, display name, email address and profile photo URL. We use these details for account access, player identification and supported profile features, not to obtain your Google password.

Guest Mode does not require signing in with Google. Google and Firebase also process information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Guest Mode, Local Storage and Progress Copy

Guest Mode stores relevant progress and settings locally on the device. Guest progress may be lost if you clear the Game’s data, uninstall the app, or change devices without a supported transfer method.

When you switch from a Google account to Guest Mode, the Game may offer to copy the highest unlocked level into Guest Mode. This is optional: declining keeps Guest Mode progress unchanged. Accepting does not lower an existing higher Guest level, and does not transfer every account detail, such as all coins or other account data.

Copying a level into Guest Mode does not itself erase your signed-in account’s cloud data. Clearing device storage does not necessarily remove cloud-stored information.

5. Player Profiles, Photos, Frames and Leaderboards

The Game may display your Google-provided name and profile photo in your own profile or leaderboard interface, along with an available decorative photo frame. A frame is a visual customization; the Game does not need to access your device camera merely to display a Google-provided profile image.

The Game includes daily and weekly ranking interfaces. Some rankings may be marked “Coming Soon” or otherwise unavailable in a particular version. If online rankings are enabled, leaderboard entries may display a player name, score or rank, and—only where implemented—an avatar or decorative frame to other players. Only the information actually shown in a leaderboard is visible to other players; account email addresses are not intended as leaderboard fields.

Avoid using personally sensitive information as a display name. Contact us if you have concerns about profile or leaderboard visibility. Account deletion and removal requests are described below.

The Game is free to play and uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video advertisements. Rewarded ads may offer in-game benefits, such as coins or extra lives, when the reward conditions are met.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, interactions such as app launches and ad views, and performance or diagnostic data. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

See Google Advertising Technologies for additional details. We do not promise that all ads are personalized or that all advertising stops when a player declines personalized advertising.

7. Ad Privacy and Consent

Where required by applicable law or advertising-platform rules, the Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present consent choices. The in-game Ad Privacy Options control may let eligible players review or change their choices.

Advertising may be limited or unavailable depending on consent status, available ads and regional requirements. Android settings may also let you control or reset advertising identifiers. If you cannot access a privacy choice that should be available, contact us.

We use information as needed to provide and improve gameplay, authenticate accounts, load or synchronize progress, operate guest saves, show profiles or supported rankings, credit earned in-game rewards, display and measure ads, maintain security, prevent misuse, respond to requests, and comply with legal obligations.
