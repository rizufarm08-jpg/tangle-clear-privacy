1. Introduction

Rizu Apps ("we", "us" or "our") publishes Clear the Arrows: Tap Puzzle (Android package: com.tangleclear.app).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you play our game, sign in, save progress, view advertisements, or contact us.

2. Information We Process

Depending on which game features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Account information: Google Sign-In identifier, email address, and available profile information used for authentication and account management.
  • Game progress: Completed levels and associated account-based progress stored using Firebase.
  • Local game information: Preferences, progress or other game state saved on your device using browser-based local storage.
  • Advertising information: IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device or advertising identifiers, and ad interaction information processed by Google AdMob.
  • Technical information: App interaction and diagnostic information processed by the advertising SDK.
  • Support communications: Information you choose to provide when emailing us about support or deletion.

3. Google Sign-In and Firebase

Clear the Arrows uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to identify signed-in players.

Firebase Cloud Firestore is used to store account-associated game progress. This helps players restore completed levels and continue playing after signing in again.

Google and Firebase process information under their applicable service and privacy policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security .

4. Local Storage

The game may store information locally on your device to support gameplay, preferences and saved state.

This information is generally retained on the device until changed or cleared. Clearing app data or uninstalling the game may remove locally stored information.

Local storage is separate from cloud data associated with a signed-in account.

5. Advertising and AdMob

Clear the Arrows uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP addresses and inferred approximate location
  • Advertising IDs and other device identifiers
  • App launches, taps and ad interactions
  • Technical and diagnostic information

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Learn more: Google Advertising Technologies .

6. Advertising Consent and Choices

Where required, the game presents advertising consent options using Google's User Messaging Platform.

Eligible players can review or update their advertising privacy preferences using the game's Ad Privacy Options feature.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that all advertising will stop. Limited or non-personalized advertising may still be permitted, depending on applicable requirements.

Android also provides controls for advertising identifiers and related privacy settings.

7. Why Information Is Used

  • To authenticate players and manage accounts
  • To store and restore completed game progress
  • To provide and maintain game functionality
  • To display and measure advertising
  • To detect abuse and protect services
  • To respond to support and deletion requests

We use Google services including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In and Google AdMob.
