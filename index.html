Clear The Arrows — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Information we collect

  • If you sign in with a phone number, we (via Firebase Authentication) collect and verify that phone number.
  • If you sign in with Facebook, we receive your Facebook name and account identifier from Facebook Login.
  • If you continue as a guest, no account information is collected — your progress stays on your device only.
  • For signed-in accounts, we store your game progress, settings, and daily-challenge status in our database (Firebase Firestore) so it syncs across your devices.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data to select and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads for details.

How we use this information

  • To let you sign in and keep your progress synced across devices.
  • To show ads that help keep the game free.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept until you request deletion. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
