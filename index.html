Account Deletion Inside the Game

Clear the Arrows also includes an in-app account deletion request option for signed-in players.

Players can submit a deletion request through the game or use the email method on this webpage.

The email method remains available even if the player cannot access the game or their device.

An in-app deletion request may create a request record for processing. Request submission does not by itself guarantee that deletion of all backend records has completed.

What Data Is Deleted?

A verified request for deletion of a Clear the Arrows account covers account information and saved game data associated with that account.

The records identified for removal include:

  • Firebase Authentication account: The account record used for Google Sign-In authentication within Clear the Arrows.
  • Account profile information: The account identifier, associated email address and any related profile information held by the game.
  • Cloud-saved game progress: Completed levels and other account-linked saved progress stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Account-specific Firestore records: Other account-linked records identified during deletion processing, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

Deleting your Clear the Arrows game account does not delete your personal Google account or other Google services.

Information independently processed by Google, Firebase or advertising providers may also be subject to their own privacy and retention policies.

Deletion Processing and Retention

Rizu Apps reviews account deletion requests and may verify account ownership before processing them.

We aim to process verified deletion requests promptly, taking account of applicable legal requirements.

Processing involves identifying the relevant Firebase Authentication account and associated Cloud Firestore data for deletion.

Sending a request does not mean deletion has already been completed. We will communicate the outcome using the support email channel.

Limited information may be retained where legitimately necessary for:

  • Compliance with applicable laws
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Handling or documenting a deletion request
  • Other legitimate retention obligations

Any such retention will be limited to the relevant purpose and applicable requirements.

The exact retention period, where applicable, depends on the category of information and the reason for retaining it.

Request status: If you have already submitted a deletion request and want an update, contact rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com using the same email conversation.

Locally Stored Game Data

Clear the Arrows may store certain game settings, preferences or progress information locally on your Android device.

Deleting a cloud account does not necessarily remove every locally stored copy of information from a device that is no longer connected to the account.

You may remove local application data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Clearing device data and deleting a Firebase cloud account are separate operations.
