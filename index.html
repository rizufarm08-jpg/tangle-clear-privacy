Request Account Deletion

If you created a Clear the Arrows account using Google Sign-In, you can request deletion of your account and associated game data at any time.

You can submit a request without opening or reinstalling the game.

Request Account Deletion

Or email rizwanliaqat1@gmail.com with the subject Clear the Arrows - Account Deletion Request.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Send an email using the button above.
  2. Include the email address associated with your Google Sign-In account.
  3. State that you want your Clear the Arrows account and associated saved game data deleted.
  4. Rizu Apps may request reasonable verification to protect your account against unauthorized deletion.
  5. After verification, Rizu Apps will arrange deletion of the applicable account and associated data and communicate the outcome.

Never send your Google password, verification codes, or other account credentials.

Delete Your Account From the App

Clear the Arrows also provides an in-app account deletion request option for signed-in players.

You may use that option or the email method above. You do not need access to the app to send an email request.

What Data Is Covered?

A verified account-deletion request covers the account information and saved progress associated with your Clear the Arrows account.

  • Firebase Authentication account record for the game
  • Google Sign-In account identifier and related profile information held for the game
  • Cloud-saved game progress associated with the account
  • Associated account-specific Firestore data

Deleting a Clear the Arrows account does not delete your Google account.

Information independently processed by Google or advertising providers may be subject to their separate retention policies.

Deletion Processing and Retention

Account deletion requests are reviewed to verify account ownership and identify the relevant records. Submission of a request does not itself confirm that backend deletion has finished.

We will communicate the processing status through the support email conversation. We do not currently publish a fixed completion deadline.

Some limited information may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or handling the request itself. Any such retention will be limited to a legitimate purpose and applicable requirements.

Local Game Data

Some game preferences or progress may be stored locally on your device. Account deletion does not necessarily remove every local copy from a device.

You can clear local app data through Android settings or uninstall the application. Clearing local data is separate from deleting your cloud account.
